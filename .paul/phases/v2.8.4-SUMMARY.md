## What Changed

### Layout: 3-column side-by-side (no tabs)

- Window full: **1350×760** — Visual (660px) | Params (360px) | Resonators (330px)
- Window compact: **690×820** — Params (360px) | Resonators (330px), stacked REP/CHOP strip

### ResonatorPanel (new component)

- Always visible alongside params — no tab that hides the visual
- 3 columns: **MODAL BANK** | **FDN (8-tap)** | **SYMPATH. STRINGS**
- Each: toggle on/off + wet slider + 1 key param (decay / size / strings)
- Bottom strip: 4 live data boxes (velocity, temperature, Dst, proton flux)
- APVTS-attached; `setLiveData()` called from `timerCallback()` at 10fps

### HIDE VISUAL button

- Anchored bottom-right corner (`TW-158`, `TH-32`)
- Click → toggles `showVisual`, calls `setSize(showVisual ? 1350 : 690, showVisual ? 760 : 820)`
- Label switches: **HIDE VISUAL** ↔ **SHOW VISUAL**

### Bottom strip dynamic layout

- **Wide (1350px):** REP left half | CHOP right half, vertical divider at `TW/2`
- **Compact (690px):** REP full-width top (y=600) | CHOP full-width bottom (y=710), horizontal divider
- `paint()` draws correct separator + labels for each mode
- Strip background fills 160px (wide) or 220px (compact)
